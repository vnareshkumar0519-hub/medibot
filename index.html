Important Clinical Notice

Health and Safety Notice

“MediBot provides general health information for educational purposes only. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.”

Recognize Medical Emergencies – Act Without Delay

If you or someone near you experiences any of the following acute symptoms, do NOT wait for online replies. Immediately contact your local emergency services or proceed to the nearest emergency department:

•
Cardiovascular Signs

Sudden chest pain, tightness, squeezing pressure, or pain radiating to jaw, back, neck, or left arm.

•
Severe Respiratory Distress

Inability to catch breath, gasping, blue or pale lips/fingertips, or acute asthma attacks not relieved by inhalers.

•
Stroke Signs (F.A.S.T.)

Facial drooping, arm weakness or numbness, slurred speech, sudden loss of balance, or sudden severe confusion.

•
Severe Trauma & Anaphylaxis

Uncontrolled bleeding, sudden severe allergic reactions (throat swelling, hives), or sudden loss of consciousness.

About The Project

About MediBot

Empowering individuals with transparent, accessible, and privacy-conscious health education.

What is MediBot?

MediBot – Healthcare Information Assistant is a lightweight, responsive educational health website built exclusively with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It serves as an informative first stop for individuals looking to better understand symptoms, healthy living principles, nutrition fundamentals, and medical terms.

Main Purpose

The primary purpose of MediBot is to enhance everyday health literacy by making evidence-aligned medical information friendly, readable, and readily accessible without technical friction, subscriptions, or invasive data collection.

Verification Standard: All health guidance should be verified with qualified healthcare practitioners and reputable public health agencies such as the World Health Organization (WHO), CDC, and NHS.

Key Features

  • Instant Predefined Knowledge

    Immediate rule-based answers on common symptoms, nutrition, stress, and sleep.

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy

    No backend, cookies, or remote analytics. Your queries stay entirely in your local browser.

  • Fully Responsive Healthcare UI

    Crafted with accessible semantic markup, high-contrast colors, and adaptive touch-friendly layout.

  • Comprehensive Topic Modals

    In-depth, readable guides covering symptoms, chronic diseases, exercise, and drug safety.

Critical Limitations

  • No Clinical Diagnostic Capability: MediBot cannot run lab tests, perform physical exams, or diagnose any medical ailment.
  • No Prescription Guidance: MediBot never provides tailored drug dosages, modifications to prescribed therapies, or off-label recommendations.
  • Local Educational Rules Only: MediBot utilizes predefined JavaScript rules and does not connect to third-party generative models or live electronic medical records (EMR).
  • Not for Emergency Interventions: Digital assistants are not suitable for urgent trauma, acute chest pain, or poisoning.
